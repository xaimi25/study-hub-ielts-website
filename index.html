The Four IELTS Modules, Covered Properly

IELTS tests four skills. Study Hub trains each one with its own strategies, timed drills and marked practice — not just general English.

Listening

30 minutes · 40 questions

Four recordings, played once. We train prediction, signpost words and spelling accuracy so you stop losing easy marks.

Reading

60 minutes · 40 questions

Three long passages. Skimming, scanning and True/False/Not Given technique, practised against the clock every week.

Writing

60 minutes · 2 tasks

Task 1 graphs and letters, Task 2 essays. Structure, band-7+ vocabulary and weekly marked scripts with corrections.

Speaking

11–14 minutes · 3 parts

A face-to-face interview. Daily speaking practice, cue-card drills and one-to-one mock interviews with band feedback.

IELTS Questions, Answered

What is the difference between IELTS Academic and General Training?

Academic is for university admission and professional registration — its Reading and Writing tasks use academic texts and topics. General Training is for immigration and work abroad, with everyday texts and a letter-writing Task 1. Listening and Speaking are the same in both. If you are unsure which one your visa or university needs, bring your case details to the demo class and we will confirm it.

What is IELTS Life Skills, and who needs it?

Life Skills is a shorter Speaking-and-Listening-only test at CEFR levels A1, A2 and B1, used mainly for UK family, spouse and settlement visas. It is graded Pass or Fail rather than with band scores. Our Life Skills batches run 2 weeks (fast track) or 4–6 weeks (standard).

How is the overall band score calculated?

Each module is scored from 1 to 9. Your overall band is the average of the four, rounded to the nearest half band — an average ending in .25 rounds up to the next half band, and .75 rounds up to the next whole band. Try it with your mock scores in the calculator above.

How long is an IELTS result valid?

IELTS Test Report Forms are normally valid for two years from the test date. Universities and immigration authorities may set their own shorter windows, so plan your test date around your application deadline.

I have average English. Which program should I join?

The 2-month Standard Program. It adds Spoken English support alongside IELTS training, so your base improves while you learn exam technique. If your level assessment shows strong English, the 1-month Fast Track gets you exam-ready sooner.
